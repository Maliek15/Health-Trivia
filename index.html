Health Trivia

How many bones does the average adult human have?

Most adults have 206 bones. Babies are born with more, and some bones fuse together as they grow.

What is the largest organ in the human body?

The skin is the body's largest organ. It helps protect the body and regulate temperature.

About how long does a red blood cell live?

A red blood cell lives for about 120 days on average before the body replaces it.

How does the stomach protect itself from its own acid?

A protective lining produces mucus and bicarbonate, which help shield the stomach wall from acid and digestive enzymes.

How many chambers does the human heart have?

The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

What do the lungs do with oxygen when you breathe?

Oxygen passes from the lungs into the bloodstream, while carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the lungs to be breathed out.

Which organ helps control blood sugar by making insulin?

The pancreas makes insulin, a hormone that helps regulate the amount of glucose in the blood.
